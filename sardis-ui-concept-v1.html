Identity

Logo concept lab

Three directions explored for the Sardis mark. The selected concept is Concept C — Intelligence Core: minimal, geometric, readable at favicon size, and stable on black or white — exactly what a global personal-intelligence brand needs.

Explorations

selecting the strongest mark
Explored
Concept A — S Monogram
Geometric S

A clean geometric "S" built as a continuous stroke. Instantly readable, but common across many AI brands and slightly generic.

Explored
Concept B — Lydian Future
Sardis lineage, modern

A nod to Sardis' Lydian history as an abstract faceted core — heritage without any antique-coin or crypto feel.

Selected
Concept C — Intelligence Core
S + core · recommended

An "S" sweeps into a fixed intelligence core — memory + focus in one mark. Legible at 16px, works on black & white.

Why C? It is the only direction that (1) reads as the letter S yet also as an active core, (2) survives favicon/app-icon sizes, (3) avoids the generic "AI S" and any coin/token reading, and (4) translates the three brand pillars — Remember · Understand · Act — into a single calm symbol.
